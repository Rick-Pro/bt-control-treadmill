Stride is a small Android remote for PitPat walking pads. It connects to the pad over Bluetooth, shows the numbers that matter, and gets out of the way.

What it does

Start, pause, stop and speed in one tap

Big buttons where your thumb already is. Speed up or down one step at a time, or jump to a preset. STOP is wide, red and always first in line.

Live time, distance, calories, steps

Straight from the pad, in mph or km. The screen stays on while the belt moves. If the link drops, the phone buzzes and tells you the belt is still running.

Every walk saved on your phone

Each walk lands in History with its time, distance, average speed and a speed chart. Weekly and all-time totals. Delete what you do not want. Share as CSV.

No account. No internet. No ads. The app has no internet permission at all. Read the privacy policy.

Install

  1. Download. On the phone, open the latest release and tap the APK file.
  2. Allow installs from your browser. Android asks once. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back.
  3. Let Play Protect scan it. Tap Scan app, wait a few seconds, then Install.
  4. Set it up once. Close the PitPat app, turn the pad on, open Stride and tap Find. Tap the pad when it shows up. Every later launch reconnects on its own.
